This incident is a look the person asked for (`look_requested`), not a failure Bleep saw; its `via` names where they asked, not a part of the service. When nothing collected shows a failure, `root_cause` says that nothing is wrong and what shows the service is healthy, `category` is `unknown`, `confidence` rates how sure you are that it is healthy, and `missing` stays empty unless something you needed could not be read. Nothing is wrong only when the service's own containers run, its own logs show no failure and every URL check passed; a failed URL check or errors in its own logs is a failure to diagnose even when every container is healthy, and another service's logs, such as a proxy's, show nothing about this one.
